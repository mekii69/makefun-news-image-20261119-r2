# Thursday 19 November 2026 — The World in One Day (r2)

One 1024x1024 front page summing up Thursday 19 November 2026:
33 stories — 13 illustrated (3 generated flat illustrations + 10
code-drawn panels), 20 numbered briefs, a world map with numbered pins,
a region strip, a stat strip, and a date check. Every story was
reported on 19 November 2026; other days' news is listed as left out.

Run: `python3 main.py` (stdlib only) writes `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
